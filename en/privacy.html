Introduction

Clean Guard is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of its clients. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use our services or visit our website.

Data Collection

Personal Data

We collect the following personal data:

  • Name and contact details (phone, email)
  • Address and service area
  • Information regarding the service you require
  • Service history and preferences

Technical Data

  • IP address and browsing information
  • Device type and browser
  • Cookies and similar technologies

Data Use

We use your data to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Communicate with you for scheduling and follow-up
  • Send important updates and information
  • Fulfil legal obligations
  • Improve website user experience
  • Statistical analysis and market research

Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption of data in transit and storage
  • Restricted access for authorised personnel only
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Secure data management and deletion procedures

Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access to your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate information
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten)
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

Cookies

We use cookies to improve your experience on our website. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

For more information, see our Cookie Policy.
