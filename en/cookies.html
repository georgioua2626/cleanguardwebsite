Cookie Policy

How we use cookies to improve your experience.

Last updated: 2/6/2026

What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

Clean Guard uses cookies to provide better services and understand how you use our website.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies
Always Active

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be disabled.

  • Maintaining your session
  • Security and protection against attacks
  • Contact form functionality
  • Language preferences
Functional Cookies
Optional

They improve the functionality of the website and the user experience.

  • Saving user preferences
  • Content customisation
  • Improved navigation
  • Chat and communication support
Analytical Cookies
Optional

They help us understand how you use our website so we can improve it.

  • Traffic statistics
  • User behaviour analysis
  • Content improvement
  • Website performance measurement

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that place their own cookies:

  • Google Analytics: For traffic statistics and analysis
  • Google Maps: For displaying maps and locations
  • reCAPTCHA: For protection against spam and bots

Managing Cookies

Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Accept or reject all cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Notification before accepting cookies
  • Exceptions for specific websites

Instructions for Popular Browsers:

Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies

Firefox: Preferences → Privacy and Security

Safari: Preferences → Privacy

Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, we will ask for your consent to use optional cookies. You can:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Choose specific categories
  • Reject all optional cookies
  • Change your preferences at any time

Data Retention

Cookies are retained for different periods:

Session Cookies

Deleted when you close your browser

Persistent Cookies

Retained for 1–24 months depending on the type

Policy Updates

We may update this cookie policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
