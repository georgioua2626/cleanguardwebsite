Who We Are

Clean Guard is a Cypriot company specialising in the protection of properties from pests and harmful organisms. With over 6 years of experience, we have protected thousands of homes and businesses throughout Cyprus.

Our mission is to provide safe, effective and eco-friendly solutions for dealing with all types of pests. We use only certified products and modern techniques.

Our trained staff hold all the necessary certifications and continue their professional development to remain up-to-date with the latest advancements in the field.
